Queue — a mode of the trailing column — Tier 1

Try: the column opens with ⌥⌘U or the queue button in the player (here it is open on load) · Return / double-click a row in “Warm-up”: it plays and the rows after it become From “Warm-up” (Play Next items stay) · right-click rows in the table → Play Next / Add to Queue · in the queue: click a row, ↑/↓, ⌫ removes (Undo in the status bar), drag a row by its handle to reorder (drop line), double-click to jump to it, right-click for its menu · Clear · Save as Playlist… · the player’s Next button skips rows that can’t play and says so.

State switcher: default · Next empty · everything empty · playback not available. Drive → Not connected: the queue is kept and says why it is paused.

Decisions: DEC-006 (queue is a column mode, not a destination), DEC-039 (menu order, no Remove from Library here), DEC-045 (skip unplayable). Old V-QUEUE elements E01–E05 keep their IDs.

Getting tracks into the queue by dragging

Drop on the player P-QUEUE.N11 D-LIB-TO-QUEUE

The toolbar player is a drop target even when the Queue column is closed: drop = Play Next. Status bar: Playing next: 3 tracks · Undo.

Drop on the Queue column P-QUEUE.N12

Two halves, one rule. Top half (Now playing and above the first row): the whole area highlights and the drop is Play Next. Bottom half: an insertion line follows the pointer between rows and the drop is Add to Queue at that place; below the last queued row it appends. Dropping on an empty queue starts nothing — it queues.

Dragging rows out of the queue D-QUEUE-ROWS

Queue and History rows drag like any track: onto a sidebar playlist (append), a sync profile, or to Finder as files. In the live window, drag a Next row onto a playlist in the sidebar.

When something can’t play

Unplayable items are skipped P-QUEUE.N13

Rows that can’t play stay visible in Next with their state in words, dimmed. When playback reaches them it moves on and reports once (DEC-045). Press the player’s Next button in the live window to see it.

Queue with the drive not connected P-QUEUE.N14

Playback pauses; the queue and the position are kept. One line at the top says why; rows are dimmed without a per-row status (the banner speaks, DEC-014). Not-downloaded rows keep their own word. Editing the queue still works.

Empty states V-QUEUE.E04

Sentences that say how to fill it. No full-pane placeholder: the three section titles stay so the column keeps its shape.
