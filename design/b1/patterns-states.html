A state is a sentence, placed once P4 · DEC-014

A state is written in words at the highest level it applies to — and only there. Lower levels go quiet instead of repeating it. One unplugged drive is one banner, not 12,000 red chips.

Text, never colour alone DEC-043 · THOUGHTS §5

Every state is a word. A symbol may accompany it; colour only supports it. No unlabeled dots, no chips: status is plain .secondary text plus an SF Symbol.
