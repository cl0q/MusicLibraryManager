Toolbar — constant in every section

The toolbar at three window widths P-TOOLBAR

The items never change with the section (DEC-048): sidebar toggle · back/forward · Add · player · Activity · Info · search. When the window gets narrow, things give way in a fixed order: 1. the player’s title/artist column, 2. the Activity text, 3. the Add button (moves into the overflow menu »). Transport, scrubber, queue button, Info and search never collapse. Widths below are the toolbar beside a 240 pt sidebar.

P-TOOLBAR.E04 (Shuffle, Re-scan Library) and P-TOOLBAR.E05 (n folders) are gone from the toolbar: Shuffle is Playback ▸ Shuffle All Tracks, ⌘R refreshes the current view, counts are in the status bar.

Activity item P-ACTIVITY

Three states, never only a colour (DEC-005, DEC-044). The text is the operation’s own short line; with several jobs it is the one that started first plus +2.

Click = popover; ⌥⌘0 = Activity window. Full design: activity.html.

Tips — three, one at a time

Space preview V-MAIN-LAYOUT.N01

Shown once, anchored to the first selected row the first time a track list has focus.

Press Space to previewSelect a track and press Space to hear it from its best part. Press Space again to go back to what was playing. Return plays it for real.

Drop on sidebar playlists V-MAIN-LAYOUT.N02

Shown once, anchored to the Playlists header, after the user has added tracks to a playlist through a menu twice.

Drag tracks onto a playlistDrop the selection on any playlist in the sidebar to add it. Drop on “Playlists” to make a new playlist.

⌘I V-MAIN-LAYOUT.N03

Shown once, anchored to the ⓘ toolbar button, the first time two or more tracks are selected.

Edit several tracks at oncePress ⌘I to show Info for the selection. With several tracks selected, what you type applies to all of them.

Rules for tips

Never more than one tip on screen. A dismissed tip never returns; using the feature dismisses its tip. No tip appears while a sheet is open or a preview is playing. Reset Tips is in Settings ▸ Advanced. No other tips are planned.
