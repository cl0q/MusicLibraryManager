Sheets & alerts — Tier 3 pattern catalogue

What this is: the rules for sheets, popovers, alerts, status-bar messages and system file panels, then every sheet (S-, 33) and alert (A-, 28) of today’s app as one card: Was (today, one sentence) · Now (kept / reworded / merged / replaced / removed, with the decision) · the redesigned surface drawn statically with its final copy. The tag at the right of each card title is the verdict. The last section lists the sheets and alerts that are new in this design. Each card links to the page where the surface can be tried.

Try: press A to see IDs, notes, decisions and SwiftUI APIs on every drawn surface; C to comment. Nothing here opens — the interactive versions live on the linked pages.

Tally of the 61: 34 stay as a sheet, alert, popover or system panel (14 kept, 20 reworded) · 8 are merged into another surface · 15 are replaced by something lighter (undoable status-bar message, inline sentence, inline rename, popover, pushed view, in-window setup) · 3 are removed · 1 is system-owned and unchanged. Decisions: DEC-016 (status bar instead of toasts and alerts), DEC-041 (undo scope), DEC-018, DEC-023, DEC-026, DEC-027, DEC-030, DEC-031, DEC-032, DEC-034, DEC-044 · THOUGHTS §3.2, §4.6, §7.20.

Rules

Which surface? THOUGHTS §3.2

Pick by what the user is doing, not by how much there is to show. If two rows fit, the upper one wins.

Sheet anatomy THOUGHTS §7.20

The title is the task. One primary button, named by its verb and the count. Errors appear above the buttons, in the sheet, in words.

1Title = the task and its object, not the app or a noun (“Import Playlist”).2First sentence = what will happen, in numbers. No instructions the controls already give.3Errors inline, above the buttons, as a sentence with the cause. Never a second alert on top of a sheet.4Footer left: one quiet remark at most (where the result goes, “You can undo this”).5Esc = Cancel. Cancel always works, also while the sheet is checking something.6Return = the one primary button: verb + count (Add 38 Tracks, Import 44 Tracks, Create and Add 3 Tracks). Disabled until valid — never a sheet that closes and then fails.

Progress: in the sheet or in Activity DEC-044

A sheet shows progress only for work that ends within about 10 seconds (checking a link, reading a playlist, comparing a folder). Anything longer is handed to Activity: the sheet closes, the status bar says that it started, and the place it belongs to echoes it.

Alert anatomy THOUGHTS §7.20

The title asks the question. The message states the consequence in numbers. The buttons are verbs; the destructive one has the destructive role. An alert never says OK to a choice.

Remove 14 tracks from the library?

Their files move to the Trash and they are removed from 3 playlists and 1 sync profile. You can put the files back from the Trash.

TTitle = the question, with name and count: Delete “Warm-up”? — not Delete playlist?.MMessage = the consequence: what is removed, what stays, how to get it back. Running work that will stop is listed, not asked about.BButtons are verbs (Move to Trash, Restore and Relaunch, Disconnect). OK appears only where there is nothing to choose.KEsc = Cancel, always. Return = the default button: the primary action when it is harmless (Switch and Relaunch, Try Again); when the action is destructive, Cancel is the default and the destructive button must be clicked.EA failure is an alert only when MLM can’t continue without the answer (Restore didn’t finish). Every other failure is a sentence where it happened.

What became an undoable status-bar message DEC-016 · DEC-041

If it can be undone it is not asked — it is done, said in the status bar for 8 s with Undo, and named in Edit ▸ Undo. Toasts and floating panels are gone.

Where failures go THOUGHTS §4.9

A failure is a sentence with the cause and one action, shown where the user is looking. The raw error text is behind Details or in the log.
