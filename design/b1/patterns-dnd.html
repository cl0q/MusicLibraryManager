Drag & drop — Tier 3 pattern catalogue

What this is: (a) the rules every drag follows; (b) one matrix of what can be dragged onto what; (c) every drag & drop entry of the inventory — the 15 that exist today and the 35 a user would expect — each with what it was, what it becomes and a drawn fragment of the drop feedback; (d) three step strips for the drags of the daily loop. The fragments are drawings, not live drags; live simulations are on the linked pages (playlists.html “Simulate drags”, queue.html, shell.html). Press A to see IDs, C to comment.

Decisions: DEC-040 (payload: internal IDs + file URLs; sidebar rows accept drops), DEC-016 / DEC-041 (status-bar confirmation with Undo), DEC-003 (playlist folders, manual order), DEC-027 (sync profiles as drop targets), DEC-018 (links), DEC-014 (drive not connected). THOUGHTS §4.5.

Result: of the 35 expected-but-missing drags, 34 are added and 1 stays out of scope (D-REMOTE-PLAYLIST-TO-SIDEBAR). Of the 15 existing ones, 12 are kept, 2 change (track payload, sidebar rows accept the drop) and 1 is a duplicate entry merged into the sidebar rule; none is lost.

(a) Rules

What a drag carries PATTERN-DND.N01 DEC-040

Tracks, albums, playlists and folders are Transferable with two representations: an internal one (IDs — used inside MLM, keeps order and the source container) and public.file-url for local files, so the same drag works inside MLM and into Finder, Mail or DJ software. Today the drag carries only a private ID (com.musiclibrary.trackdrag, not even declared), so nothing outside MLM understands it.

Drop feedback PATTERN-DND.N02

Four signals, all system-drawn. Onto something: the row, card or well gets the accent ring. Between rows: an insertion line. The drag image is the first dragged row with a count badge. The cursor says what will happen: + = added / copied (the source keeps it), plain arrow = moved or reordered, “not allowed” = this place does not take it — such rows show no ring at all.

A drop changes something without opening anything, so it always says what happened — in the status bar, with Undo for 8 s and in Edit ▸ Undo. Nothing navigates, the open view does not switch. What was skipped is counted in the same sentence.
